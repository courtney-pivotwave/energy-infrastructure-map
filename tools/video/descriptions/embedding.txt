Put the live Strategic Energy Infrastructure Map on your own site: a map-only view for articles, blogs and classrooms, in one line of HTML.

Copy the embed code (with region options) from the About page:
https://strategicenergymap.org/embed

Or paste this into your page:

<iframe src="https://strategicenergymap.org/?embed=1" width="100%" height="480" style="border:0" title="Strategic Energy Infrastructure Map" loading="lazy"></iframe>

To choose where it opens, add &region=europe, &region=mideast, &region=asia or &region=americas to the address, for example:
https://strategicenergymap.org/?embed=1&region=mideast

The embedded map stays current as the map updates every day. Free, with no ads, no cookies and no tracking across sites.
